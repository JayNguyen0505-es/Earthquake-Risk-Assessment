About the project

Connecting data,
research, and decisions.

VAST-Earthquake brings station metadata, waveform information, and earthquake research into one system for the Northwest Vietnam study area.

The project connects research on earthquake parameter estimation and ground-shaking prediction with planned assessment and response workflows. Analysis and assessment features will become available as the research models are integrated.
